## TL;DR
We build Takar: a POS, kitchen and bar stations, and recipe-level stock tracking, on Fariz's Possum codebase, sold as its own brand with Ziggy Lounge as the first customer.
- Problem. ESB makes Toper do data work across two UIs and many master-data screens, small ingredients aren't tracked, deliveries and counts get skipped, and he has been burned by untracked deletions before.
- Solution. Every sale moves stock to the gram through a recipe tree (ingredient, prep item, menu item, options). Voids, discounts and adjustments need a manager PIN and land in a log nobody can edit. The POS keeps selling offline.
- Deliverables now. This PRD, plus a Figma file with 8 feature sections (stories, flows, 19 screens and a receipt, a clickable prototype) and a design system page.
- Next. Answer the open questions with Toper, have Fariz size phase 1 against Possum, and decide on the ESB renewal by 15 Nov 2026.

## Solution overview
Takar is one app with three device modes over one stock ledger. Every sale, prep batch, delivery, count and waste entry is a movement in grams, millilitres or pieces, so stock on hand is always the sum of what happened.

Runs on |

What it can do
 |

Cashier tablet | 
Cashier, waiter | 
Take orders, send to stations, take payment, open and close shift. Nothing else.
 | 
Station | 
Kitchen tablet, bar tablet | 
Kitchen crew, barista | 
See tickets for their station, mark ready, make prep batches, log waste, quick count

Back office | 
Laptop (and phone for the manager) | 
Manager, inventory admin, head chef | 
Menu and recipes, ingredients, purchasing, counts, reports, audit log, team
The recipe tree. Everything an outlet sells is built from three kinds of items:
- Ingredient, bought from a supplier. Stocked in one base unit (g, ml or pcs) and bought in packs: fresh milk is stocked in ml and bought as a carton of 12 × 1 L.
- Prep item, made in-house from ingredients. It has a batch recipe and a yield: one batch of ayam ungkep uses 2 kg chicken, 40 g garlic, 20 g palm sugar and 15 g salt, and yields 20 portions. Prep items are stocked, so a sale uses up a portion, and the raw chicken was already used when the batch was made.
- Menu item, sold to guests. Its recipe lines point at ingredients or prep items, in any compatible unit. Options change the recipe: an add-on adds lines (extra egg), a swap replaces one (oat milk for fresh milk), a level scales one (less sugar = half the syrup).
When a ticket is sent to a station, Takar walks the tree and moves stock for every line. One Ice Matcha Latte, large, oat milk, less sugar, uses 5 g matcha, 195 ml oat milk, 8 ml cane syrup, 200 g ice, one 22 oz cup, one lid and one straw.
Three rules make it trustworthy:
- Stock moves when the ticket is sent, not at payment, because that is when the drink is made. A void after sending counts as waste unless the station confirms it was never made.
- Nothing is ever deleted. A correction is a new movement with a reason, a person and, where needed, a manager's PIN.
- "Can make" for each menu item is the lowest count its ingredients allow. At or below the par level the item shows Running low; when one more can't be made it shows Sold out on the POS by itself. A manager can override with a PIN, and the override is logged.

recipe tree for Ziggy Fried Rice · ingredients, prep, menu item, options
Ziggy Fried Rice draws on stocked prep (ayam ungkep, sambal) and raw ingredients (egg, shallot, kecap manis); its options add, scale or remove single lines, and its full recipe costs Rp14.440 a plate.

Rules
- Units. Mass (g), volume (ml) and count (pcs) convert within themselves. Crossing them needs a value on the ingredient, such as "1 egg = 55 g". Amounts are stored to 0.01 of the base unit.
- Cost. POC uses the latest purchase price per base unit; moving average comes later. Prep cost = batch cost ÷ yield. Menu cost = sum of its lines, options included.
- When stock moves. On send to station. A void before sending moves nothing; a void after sending is waste unless the station confirms the item was never made.
- Can make. The lowest whole number of portions any recipe line allows from stock on hand, looking through sub-recipes that are not stocked themselves.
- Running low and sold out. An ingredient at or below par raises an alert. A menu item shows Running low at 5 or fewer it can make (set per item) and Sold out below 1.
- Negative stock only happens through a manager override, is shown in red and asks for a count.
- Sanity check. A delivery over 5× or under 1/5 of the median of the last 5 deliveries of that ingredient needs a second confirmation.
- Count maths. Expected = last approved count + movements since. Gap = counted − expected, valued at current cost.
Non-functional
- Offline. POS and stations keep selling and printing to LAN printers without internet; menu and stock are cached; queued sales sync in time order when the connection returns.
- Speed. Search under 300 ms and ticket to station under 2 seconds on a mid-range 10-inch Android tablet.
- Hosting. Supabase, as Fariz proposed. The free plan caps storage at 2 GB, so the pilot should run on Pro (about US$25 a month), with photos compressed and old delivery-note images archived yearly.
- Language. Bahasa Indonesia and English, using one plain-word glossary across POS, station and back office.
## Design direction and Figma deliverables
Takar looks quiet on purpose: light grey and white, ink for actions, one lime accent, and pastel cards for headline numbers, while the customer's own brand and photos carry the rest. The logo is a T that pours: one lime drop for the measured amount.
- Name. Takar, from takaran, a measured amount. Tagline: "Every gram, accounted for" (Setiap gram tercatat).
- Colour. Ink #1B1D21 for primary actions; lime #D3F26A for the active item and the drop in the logo. Lavender, sky and mint tint headline numbers, and teal marks the one card that asks for action. Stock bars are green, amber at par and red when empty.
- Type. Plus Jakarta Sans, drawn for the city of Jakarta, in one family from captions to totals.
- Principles. Defaults over choices (Dine in and the next pager number are preset). One place to edit (no master-data detours). Show the gram (every option says what it does to stock). Touch first: 56 px actions on POS, dark mode for stations.
- References. Draft 1 took its layout from your two POS screenshots. Draft 2 takes its look from the Niond dashboard you shared: white cards on grey, a lime active item, pastel numbers and less on each card. Icons come from your Hugeicons pack.
